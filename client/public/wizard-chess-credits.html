How the scene was prepared

The character GLBs are unchanged copies of the supplied models. For faster browser loading, the four stone piece models were simplified to about 5% of their original triangle counts, centered on their bases, joined into one mesh each, and Draco-compressed. The game adds lighting, colors, and costume details at runtime.

The playable board follows chess consultant Jeremy Silman’s documented endgame. The finished film cut intermediate moves, so this scene uses his legal composed line rather than claiming frame-perfect film continuity.
